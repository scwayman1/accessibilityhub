## 1. What the product does (and does not do)

Coastline Accessibility Hub reviews course documents (PDFs) for technical accessibility signals, explains each finding, applies permission-gated fixes **to a copy** (never the original), and rechecks the copy. Every result is routed to one of four lanes — **Needs attention**, **Review recommended**, **Verified signal**, **Not assessed**. The product produces technical evidence and review routing only. It does not issue a compliance score, certification, or overall pass, and it is not a WCAG, Section 508, PDF/UA, or legal conformance determination.

The review engine is deterministic and works without artificial intelligence. No AI model is enabled in the hosted service.

## 3. How we use information

Only to provide the service to you and your institution: authenticate you, scan uploads for malware, run the deterministic review, apply the fixes you approve to a copy, show results, keep a content-free audit trail, prevent abuse, honor deletion requests, and respond to support and security incidents.

We do **not**: sell personal information or documents; use documents or student information for advertising, profiling, or building non-educational profiles; or use documents to train AI models.

## 4. Artificial intelligence

- The hosted service has model access switched off (`HUB_BYOK_MODEL_ENABLED=false`, `HUB_MODEL_EGRESS_ENABLED=false` in `render.real-intake.yaml`).
- The local workbench includes an **optional** "bring your own key" connection that a user may configure themselves. If configured, nothing is sent until the user reviews an on-screen manifest of exactly what will and will not be sent and clicks **Allow once**. The key is held in memory only.
- Any future model use in the hosted service requires explicit, per-document, per-provider, per-purpose opt-in consent that can be revoked (`service/real_intake/consent.py`), advance notice to the institution, and an update to the subprocessor list.

## 5. Sponsored messages

While a review is processing, the hosted workspace may display a "Sponsored" card for the Coastline College Foundation. These cards are rendered by our own server from a static file; they use no scripts, pixels, cookies, or tracking, are not a link in the hosted workspace, and never delay results. **No account, student, or document data is shared with any sponsor.** In the local workbench, the sponsor message includes an ordinary link to `https://www.coastline.edu`; following it is a normal browser navigation and carries no document or account data.

## 6. Sharing and subprocessors

We share information only with service providers that process it on our behalf under written obligations (see the subprocessor table in `ferpa-dpa-template.md`): Render (hosting, Oregon, US), Clerk (authentication), and a private object-storage provider [TO BE SELECTED]. Malware scanning uses ClamAV that we host ourselves; documents are not sent to an outside scanning service. We may also disclose information when legally required, with notice to the institution where permitted.

## 7. Retention and deletion

See `data-retention-and-deletion.md`. Summary: documents are kept until the document owner deletes them (current policy `manual-owner-deletion-only`), or as the institution's agreement specifies. Deletion removes the original, clean copy, fixed copies, evidence, jobs, findings, and consents, then verifies their absence. A content-free deletion record and audit events are kept to prove the deletion happened. Backups expire on the provider's schedule [MAXIMUM BACKUP-EXPIRY DELAY — TO BE DOCUMENTED].

## 8. Security

Summary (details in `hecvat-lite-draft.md`): owner-scoped access enforced by Postgres row-level security; malware scan before any parsing; private network services with no public datastore endpoints; HTTPS on a single exact origin; required multi-factor authentication for the operator account; append-only audit records. ## 9. Students and FERPA

The product is designed for educators, not students. When an institution uses it with documents that contain education records, we act as a "school official" under the institution's direct control per the institution's Data Processing Agreement, which governs over this policy.
