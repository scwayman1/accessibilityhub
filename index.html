One model on every path

Review. Understand. Improve. Verify.

A focused review surfaces concrete accessibility signals, explains their student impact, gives one practical next step, and keeps educator judgment visible. The same four steps guide both the safe sample and local reviewer.

01 / REVIEW

See clear signals

Start with evidence about document basics such as title, primary language, structure, text, images, and links.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Connect evidence to impact

Learn who may be affected, what the signal can establish, and where human context is still needed.

03 / IMPROVE

Take one clear action

Fix a supported detail in a copy or carry the evidence back to the source document.

04 / VERIFY

Check the specific change

Compare before and after evidence without turning individual signals into an overall determination.

Helpful, not overconfident

Clear next steps, without a false “all clear.”

Some document details can be checked directly. Others depend on meaning, context, and the people who created the material. Coastline keeps that difference visible.

  • Document issues stay grouped by what needs attention, what needs review, and what could not be checked.
  • A specific signal is not a legal, certification, or conformance determination.
  • Supported updates are made to a copy and checked again.
  • Optional AI help, when available, stays separate and asks before it is used.
