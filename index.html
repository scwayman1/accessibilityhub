A clearer way to review course materials

See what to improve. Keep what already works.

A focused review surfaces concrete accessibility signals, gives practical next steps, and makes clear where educator context is essential. It is built for Coastline’s roughly 900 part-time faculty—alongside everyone else who shares course content.

01 / START

Choose a course PDF

Begin with a reading, worksheet, handout, or another material you share with students.

02 / LOOK

See clear signals

Check document basics such as title, primary language, structure indicators, text availability, images, and links.

03 / DECIDE

Bring your context

Meaningful images, reading order, and the right source fix still need the people who know the material.

04 / CHECK AGAIN

Make a small update

For supported details like title and language, update a copy and see which specific signals changed.

Helpful, not overconfident

Clear next steps, without a false “all clear.”

Some document details can be checked directly. Others depend on meaning, context, and the people who created the material. Coastline keeps that difference visible.

  • Document issues stay grouped by what needs attention, what needs review, and what could not be checked.
  • A specific signal is not a legal, certification, or conformance determination.
  • Supported updates are made to a copy and checked again.
  • Optional AI help, when available, stays separate and asks before it is used.
